# BGM制作指示 — ゴブリンたち vs 勇者

最低限は3曲。すべて歌なし。通常の殴り合いでは探索曲を継続し、宝を確保したら脱出曲へ切り替える。提供された3曲をv0.1.10で組み込み済み。

## 1. 待機・準備：ゴブリンの作戦会議

タイトル、ロビー、装備抽選で共用。目安2分。ファイル名：`bgm-lobby.mp3`。

```text
Instrumental fantasy party-game music, mischievous goblins preparing a ridiculous treasure heist. Playful bassoon, pizzicato strings, wooden percussion, plucked lute, light hand drums. 100 BPM, D minor with a cheeky, warm character. A memorable short three-note motif. Cozy underground hideout, funny but adventurous, never childish. Steady medium-low energy, room for conversation. Loop-friendly arrangement, short intro, no dramatic ending or fade-out. No vocals, no choir, no spoken words, no sound effects. About two minutes.
```

## 2. ダンジョン探索：巣の奥へ

通常の探索・訓練・小競り合いで共用。目安3分。ファイル名：`bgm-dungeon.mp3`。

```text
Instrumental fantasy dungeon exploration music for a playful goblins-versus-hero game. 84 BPM, D minor. Muted plucked strings, low bassoon, soft marimba, restrained hand percussion and subtle dark ambient pads. A sparse three-note motif, curiosity, hidden passages and quiet danger. Sneaky and mysterious, not horror, not a battle anthem. Low intensity with gentle variation, leaving space for footsteps and combat sounds. Loop-friendly arrangement with no big intro, climax, hard ending or fade-out. No vocals, choir, voices or sound effects. About three minutes.
```

## 3. 宝の確保後：お宝は渡さない！

宝を取って入口へ戻る間。目安2分。ファイル名：`bgm-escape.mp3`。

```text
Instrumental fantasy chase music: a hero runs for the dungeon exit carrying stolen treasure while furious goblins pursue. 144 BPM, D minor. Driving staccato strings, rhythmic hand drums, low brass accents, agile bassoon counter-melodies and plucked lute. Transform a short three-note motif into an urgent, catchy chase theme. Exciting, mischievous and slightly chaotic, with a real chance of escape. Steady forward momentum, controlled low end, no deafening wall of sound. Loop-friendly, immediate rhythmic start, no long breakdown, final hit or fade-out. No vocals, choir, voices or sound effects. About two minutes.
```

曲間を統一するため、同じ楽器と調性を指定している。曲は繰り返し再生し、場面切り替えではクロスフェードする。曲末から曲頭への完全な継ぎ目の除去は行っていない。勝利・敗北の短い曲は後回しでよい。
