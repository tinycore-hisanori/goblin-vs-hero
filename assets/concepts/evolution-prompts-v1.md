# Viewer goblin evolution concepts

Created with built-in image_gen. Reference: viewer-goblin-v1.png. These are concept illustrations for Meshy, not generated or rigged GLB assets. Evolution gameplay and numerical balance are not yet implemented.

Proposed order in this game: normal goblin → hob goblin → goblin king → goblin lord. Preserve the orange neckerchief. Proposed tradeoff: increased power and size reduce stealth and access to small burrows. Exact movement restrictions require playtesting and are not final.
